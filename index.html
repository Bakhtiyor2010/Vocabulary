About SAT Vocabulary Mastery

SAT Vocabulary Mastery is a comprehensive, spaced-repetition learning platform specifically optimized for the Reading and Writing sections of the digital SAT.

The system focuses on high-frequency Tier 2 vocabulary words that are highly likely to appear in 'Words in Context' questions on the exam.

Core Methodologies:

  • Spaced Repetition Algorithm (SRS): Adapts to your learning curve. Difficult words are reviewed more frequently to ensure long-term retention before your test date.
  • Contextual Learning: Every SAT word is provided with detailed definitions, exact synonyms, and realistic sentence examples reflecting the style of SAT passages.
  • Performance Analytics: Track your Mastered Cards and Average Easiness metric to gauge your readiness for the real exam.
  • Global Search: Instantly look up words while taking practice tests or reviewing mistakes.
  • Targeted SAT Decks: Organize words by frequency or difficulty to structure your SAT prep.

Maximize your SAT verbal score through consistent, scientifically-backed vocabulary training.
